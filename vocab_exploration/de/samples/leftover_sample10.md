## Review notes (manual)

- **4. das Dur**: genitive is wrong. *Dur* doesn't inflect, so it's **des Dur**, not *des Durs*.
- **1. die Elektronik**: the definition is "devices", but example 2 (*interessiert sich für Elektronik*) is the field of study. That's two related senses in one entry.
- **8. die Unsicherheit**: the definition is "not knowing what will happen". Example 1 (*Unsicherheit vor der Prüfung*) leans toward the feeling of being unsure. It's a small mismatch.
- Everything else (articles, plurals, translations, example naturalness) looks right.
